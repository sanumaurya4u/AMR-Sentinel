⚠️ The Critical Outpatient Consumption Paradox:

While WHO mandates ≥60% Access-group antibiotics, Indian outpatient prescribing is heavily dominated by Watch-group agents: Azithromycin (25.1 DDD/100 bed-days), Ceftriaxone (11.3 DDD), and Cefixime (11.0 DDD). These reserve molecules are routinely overprescribed for self-limiting viral coughs and uncomplicated cystitis, directly breeding multi-drug resistant superbugs.

⏱️

The Point-of-Care Bottleneck (Why Doctors Overprescribe)

OPD: Race Against Time (30–60s per Patient)
Indian outpatient clinics see 80–120 patients daily. There is literally zero time to calculate renal adjustments or cross-reference 200+ page clinical manuals.
Cognition vs. Manuals (Guideline Fatigue)
ICMR Treatment Guidelines 2022 and CDSCO banned lists are published as static PDFs that gather dust. Clinicians rely on empirical habit and pharmaceutical brand push.
Defensive Prescribing Under Diagnostic Uncertainty
Without same-day culture reports, clinicians prescribe ultra-broad Watch antibiotics "just in case", destroying gut microbiota and generating pan-drug resistance.
⚖️

Clinical Reality: Three Cases of Outpatient UTI

Conventional Allopathy

Recurrent MDR Klebsiella UTI in kidney recipient. Empiric Watch failure required salvage antibiotics & source control.

Ayurvedic Study

E. coli UTI treated with Varividaryadi Kashaya. Single-case negative culture; lacked randomized controlled trial validation.

Homeopathic Report

2 published resistant UTI cases reported symptom relief; controlled evidence remains insufficient for acute severe bacteremia.

Layer 1 • Assistive Intake Edge (Untrusted)
👁️

Multimodal Ingestion & Entity Parsing

Gemini Multimodal Vision + Local OCR + RapidFuzz

Extracts messy, handwritten prescription slips, EMR PDFs, or digital intake forms into structured clinical facts:

1
Patient Demographics & Physiologic Parameters Age band (pediatric/geriatric), sex, pregnancy status, lactation.
2
Renal Function & Organ Clearance Calculates/flags eGFR <30 or <50 mL/min/1.73m² and hepatic status.
3
Medication List & Regimen Normalization Standardized generic molecule matching, dosage, frequency, and duration.
4
Field Confidence Labeling Each extracted element carries a confidence metric (0.00 to 1.00).
👨‍⚕️

Mandatory Human Clinician Confirmation Gate

CRITICAL SAFETY LAW: AI extraction is strictly UNTRUSTED. The engine refuses to audit until a qualified clinician verifies and locks the structured facts. Missing data is NEVER assumed normal.

Layer 2 • Deterministic Clinical Audit Core
⚡

Pure Python Zero-Hallucination Audit Engine

Zero Network • Zero LLMs • Zero DB • Execution Time: <15ms

Audits locked prescription facts against immutable, versioned ICMR, WHO, and CDSCO clinical rules:

T1
Tier 1: Absolute Contraindications & Organ Toxicity Pediatric fluoroquinolones (cartilage damage), pregnancy teratogens, severe renal failure.
T2
Tier 2: Indication Legitimacy & CDSCO Banned FDCs Blocks lethal unapproved combinations (e.g. Cefixime + Azithromycin, Ofloxacin + Ornidazole).
T3
Tier 3: WHO AWaRe Spectrum De-escalation Alerts when 2nd/3rd line Watch/Reserve antibiotics are used where Access suffices.
T4
Tier 4: ICMR Short-Course Treatment Duration Limits Flags excessive duration (e.g. 14 days prescribed for uncomplicated cystitis instead of 3-5).

🚫 Strict Clinical Safety Prohibitions (What AMR Sentinel NEVER Does)

✖ NEVER diagnoses a medical disease
✖ NEVER calculates or dictates drug dosages
✖ NEVER auto-substitutes medications
✖ NEVER claims a prescription is "approved/safe"
Architectural Proof: Build-time Python Abstract Syntax Tree (AST) isolation tests guarantee zero external network calls in audit core.
